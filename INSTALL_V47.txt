QUESTION BANK REVIEW
All 60 renal questions have been reviewed and revised: 30 Clinical Medicine and 30 Medical Sciences.
42 are Standard and 18 are Harder. These are editorial difficulty estimates, not scores calibrated against student performance.
Revisions address answer-giving wording, implausible alternatives, unnecessary qualifiers and difficulty labels.
Each option now has a case-specific explanation. Questions retain the course LO, note-coverage label and source references.
Clinical guidance uses Australian references. Uploaded Kumar & Clark and Harrison textbooks support medical science.
The uploaded sample course examination was used to judge style; questions are newly written and not copied from that examination.
The set covers selected renal LOs, not every possible renal objective.
